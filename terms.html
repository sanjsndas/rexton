1. Our covenant

Rexton Muffin Whisper is a space for thoughtful exchange. By using this site, you agree to approach all content — essays, tables, recipes, and commentary — with an open mind and a gentle spirit. We reserve the right to curate, edit, or remove any contribution that disrupts the quiet.

We pledge to never use clickbait, fear-mongering, or reductive nutrition labels. You pledge to never use our content as medical advice without consulting a qualified professional.

All articles, illustrations, and data visualizations are original works of the Rexton Muffin Whisper collective. You may:

  • Read, bookmark, and share for personal, non‑commercial use

Commercial use, republishing, or scraping requires explicit written permission. For permissions, reach out via our contact page.

3. User conduct

We welcome comments, letters, and recipe feedback. However, we reserve the right to moderate or remove:

If you see something that violates these guidelines, please flag it to our editorial team.
