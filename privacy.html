Privacy Policy

Your data, our respect. We believe in transparency, minimal collection, and absolute care — just like we treat every fruit‑protein pairing.

Last updated: April 2026 Effective: May 2026 Version 2.1

Rexton Muffin Whisper (“we”, “our”, “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal data when you visit our website or interact with us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the data you voluntarily provide:

  • Contact information — name, email address, and any message you send via our forms.
  • Usage data — anonymous analytics (via cookies) to understand how our content is read.
  • Newsletter sign‑ups — email address and preferences (if you opt in).

We do not collect sensitive data, financial information, or location data without explicit consent.

Our promise: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Ever.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to your inquiries, comments, or pitches.
  • To send you our newsletter (only if you opted in).
  • To improve our content and user experience via aggregated analytics.
  • To comply with legal obligations (e.g., data protection requests).

3. Cookies & Tracking

We use minimal cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Our cookies fall into these categories:

  • Essential — necessary for site functionality.
  • Analytics — anonymous traffic tracking (we use privacy‑friendly tools).
  • Preferences — remembering your settings (e.g., theme).

You can control cookies through your browser settings. For more details, see our Cookies Policy.

4. Data Storage & Security

We store your data on secure servers with industry‑standard encryption. Access is limited to essential personnel. We retain your data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined above or as required by law.

5. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your data.
  • Rectify — correct any inaccurate data.
  • Erase — request deletion of your data.
  • Object — opt out of certain processing.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@rextonmuffin.com.

6. Third‑Party Links

Our articles may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to read their policies before sharing any personal information.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our content is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via a prominent notice on our website or by email. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates the latest revision.

“Privacy is not an option, it’s a right. We treat your data like our favorite heirloom recipe — with care, respect, and a touch of secrecy.”

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or your data, please reach out:
