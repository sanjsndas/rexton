Cookies Policy

We use cookies to make your experience tasteful and smooth — like a perfectly ripe fig. Here’s everything you need to know.

Last updated: April 2026 Effective: May 2026 Version 1.4

Rexton Muffin Whisper (“we”, “our”, “us”) uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing, analyse site traffic, and personalise content. This policy explains what they are, why we use them, and how you can control them.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences, understand how you interact with our content, and improve your experience. Think of them as a gentle whisper from our site to your browser.

Our approach: We use only minimal, privacy‑respecting cookies. No third‑party tracking for advertising. No surprises.

2. How we use cookies

We categorise our cookies as follows:

TypePurposeDuration
EssentialEnable basic site functions (e.g., navigation, secure access).Session / persistent
AnalyticsCollect anonymous usage data to improve content and structure.Up to 12 months
PreferencesRemember your settings (e.g., theme, font size).6 months

We do not use targeting/advertising cookies, nor do we share data with third‑party ad networks.

3. Your control & consent

When you first visit our site, we ask for your consent via a cookie banner. You can:

  • Accept all — enable all non‑essential cookies.
  • Reject non‑essential — only essential cookies will be set.
  • Customise — choose which categories to enable.

You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the “Cookie Settings” link in the footer or by adjusting your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies entirely.

4. Third‑Party cookies

We use a minimal set of third‑party services that may set their own cookies:

  • Google Analytics (anonymised) — to measure traffic and engagement. Data is processed under Google’s privacy policy.
  • Substack / newsletter — if you subscribe, Substack may set a cookie for authentication.

We do not control these third‑party cookies. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update our Cookies Policy to reflect changes in technology or regulation. Any material changes will be communicated via a notice on our website. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

“We believe in cookies that are warm, honest, and made from scratch — just like our content.”
